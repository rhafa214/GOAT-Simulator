Divisão de Ações do Reducer:

1. playerReducer:
- INITIALIZE_PLAYER
- TRAIN_ATTRIBUTE

2. careerReducer:
- SETUP_CAREER (atualiza currentClub, nextMatch)

3. financeReducer:
- SETUP_CAREER (atualiza weeklyWage)

4. narrativeReducer:
- ADD_NEWS

5. flowReducer:
- CHANGE_PHASE
- SET_DRAFT_LENGTH (se aplicável ao flow)
- SETUP_CAREER (muda phase para 'HUB')

6. rootReducer (Cross-cutting operations delegadas para funções puras):
- ADVANCE_WEEK (chama advanceWeekLogic)
- ADVANCE_MONTH (chama advanceWeekLogic em loop)
- RESOLVE_EVENT (chama resolveEventLogic)

GameEngine.tsx conterá:
- Contexto (GameContext)
- Provider (GameProvider)
- Hook (useGameEngine)
